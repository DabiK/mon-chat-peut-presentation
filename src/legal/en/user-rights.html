This page explains simply and transparently the rights you have over your personal data when you use the Mon Chat Peut application, published by Dabi Keita.

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights that you can exercise free of charge at any time.

1. Right of access

You can ask:

  • what data we hold about you,
  • how it is used,
  • who it is shared with,
  • how long it is kept.

We will provide a copy of the data in a clear and understandable format.

2. Right to rectification

You can:

  • correct inaccurate information,
  • complete incomplete information,
  • update your profile or your cats' profiles.

Some changes can be made directly in the application.

3. Right to erasure (right to be forgotten)

You can ask us to delete:

  • your account,
  • your personal data,
  • the data about your cats,
  • your preferences.

Deletion is permanent and results in the loss of all data synced in the application.

Some information (e.g. data about purchases made through Apple/Google) may be kept if the law requires it.

4. Right to object

You can object at any time to:

  • the use of your data for certain purposes,
  • the use of Analytics,
  • certain processing based on our legitimate interest.

This may limit some features of the application.

5. Right to restriction of processing

You can temporarily stop your data from being used, for example:

  • if the data contains an error,
  • if you contest a processing operation,
  • while a deletion request is being reviewed.

6. Right to data portability

You can ask for a copy of your data in a format that is:

  • structured,
  • commonly used,
  • machine-readable.

This includes:

  • your profile,
  • your cats,
  • your feeding history.

We will provide the data in a compatible format (e.g. JSON, CSV).

7. Right to withdraw consent

For any processing based on your consent (e.g. Analytics, AI, notifications), you can withdraw that consent at any time, without giving a reason.

This does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before.

8. Exercising your rights

How do I exercise my rights?

By email:

contact@mon-chat-peut.com

In the application: an option to delete your account is available in the settings.

Response time: we reply within 30 days at most, as required by the GDPR.

9. Right to lodge a complaint (CNIL)

If you believe your rights are not being respected, you can contact:

CNIL — Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (the French data protection authority)
Website: https://www.cnil.fr

You can file an official complaint at any time.

10. Security and confidentiality

We use modern technologies (Firebase, encryption, secure authentication) to protect your data.

However, no system is completely invulnerable.

In the event of an incident or a personal data breach, you will be informed in accordance with legal obligations.

11. Changes to this page

We may update this page to:

  • reflect new laws,
  • clarify certain points,
  • add new features to the App.

If there is a major change, we will let you know directly in the application.
