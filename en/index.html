Chocolate

in short
Don't give this food.
why
Chocolate contains theobromine and caffeine, which cats get rid of very poorly. Even a single square can cause vomiting, tremors and heart rhythm problems. The darker it is, the more dangerous.

questions

What people ask us most

Does it replace my vet?

No. The app helps with everyday decisions. If your cat has swallowed something toxic, call your vet or an animal poison control center right away, without waiting for symptoms.

How does the app recognize a dish?

Take a photo or type its name. The AI identifies the food and its likely ingredients (onion, garlic, salt…), then gives you the verdict with an explanation.

I have several cats. Does it work?

Yes. Each cat has its own profile: age, weight, goal, meal plan and tracking. Switch between them in one tap.

Is it on iPhone?

Yes. The app is available on iPhone (App Store) and Android (Google Play).

What happens to my data?

We only collect what the app needs. You can view, correct or delete your account and your cats' data at any time (GDPR).
